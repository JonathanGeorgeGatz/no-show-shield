The No-Show Shield

One HTML file, fully offline: paste your appointment export and see what no-shows and late cancellations actually cost — re-filled slots credited back, proper cancellations counted as free, the repeat offenders listed next to what they spend, and a deposit policy back-tested against your own history before you bet your goodwill on one.

What's in the bundle

  • No_Show_Shield.html — the tool; open it in any browser, nothing is uploaded anywhere
  • The three exact prompts typed on camera, and the written spec
  • Fictional sample data (Copperpaw Grooming Co. — 2,251 invented appointments, @example.com)
  • engine_v1.js — the first build, kept verbatim, bugs and all
  • checker.py — the independent Python implementation used to verify the engine

What it found on the sample business

  • The first build said missed appointments cost $12,267 a year — wrong in both directions at once: 90 of 132 real no-shows were invisible to it (spelling variants) while 199 cancellations were charged at full price, including 162 that cost nothing
  • The real number: $9,111, with $855 credited back for nine slots the desk re-filled
  • A $25 deposit on services $60 and up would have collected $1,725 — while touching 39% of bookings

How it was tested

The shipped engine was checked against an independent Python implementation on 200 generated businesses — 84,162 fields compared, zero disagreements — plus 16 functional browser tests with the network blocked and a sweep proving every setting changes the output on the shipping sample data. The wrong turns are in the episode on purpose.
